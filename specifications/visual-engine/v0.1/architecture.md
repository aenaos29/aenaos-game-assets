# Architecture

## Reusable spatial rules

- All walkable surfaces are flush at Y = 0.
- No gaps, steps or invisible snags.
- Protected pedestrian corridors are approximately 4.9 m clear along all seven radial paths.
- Entrance clearance zones are approximately 4.25 m radius.
- Validate central-square and outer-promenade lanes, spawn clearance and exact rotated building-footprint OBBs.
- Validate each prop's full physical footprint before placement.
- Add matching Rapier colliders.

## Veridian Plaza-specific decisions

Seven inward-facing buildings surround a circular pedestrian square and connect to radial walkways plus an outer promenade ring. There are no vehicle roads or cars.

The seven buildings are Courthouse, Police Station, Research Laboratory, Office Building, Lounge, School and UNION Café. Their facade features and named architectural details remain exactly as listed in the reference handoff.

Street elements include trees, cypresses, flower urns, benches, solar-ring lamps, recycling pods, courier data-drop terminals, pergola, holo-wayfinding plinth, mechanical songbirds, dust motes and distant neo-arcology skyline. This list is project-specific until independently validated as reusable.
