# Android Characters

## Reusable specifications

Avoid blocky, voxel or low-poly characters. Use realistic-proportioned articulated models.

The playable explorer has a pearl-ceramic chest carapace, carbon-fiber joints, glowing cyan wraparound optical visor, sternum reactor core, dorsal spine telemetry LEDs, shoulder pauldrons, crossbody data-pack satchel and tech scarf collar. Animation requirements are multi-jointed knee/elbow walk, jog and idle, with boot soles grounded at Y = 0.

Conversational bypassers patrol walkable corridors, pause politely and turn toward the player when approached, and cycle through three sci-fi dialogue lines when spoken to.

## Veridian Plaza-specific roster

Six bypassers are defined: EL-7 Elena, MK-4 Marcus, NR-9 Noor, JL-2 Julian, CL-5 Claire and MT-3 Mateo. Each has distinct chassis colours, LED hues, cranial crest variants (halo, antennae, fin or visor cap) and props including holopad, thermal coffee pod, briefcase and data-tote.

These identities and assignments are project-specific decisions.
