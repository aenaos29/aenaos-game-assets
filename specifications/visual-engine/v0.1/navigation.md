# Navigation

## Reusable specifications

- Single-player 3D exploration traversal.
- Smooth traversal is prioritized over gameplay complexity.
- No storylines, combat, scoring loops, investigation mechanics or timers.
- Walkable corridors must be validated before placing props or vegetation.
- Playable character boot soles are grounded at Y = 0.
- Bypassers patrol walkable corridors, pause and turn toward the player when approached.

## Veridian Plaza-specific decisions

The prototype is named Veridian Plaza and uses 16:9 landscape orientation. The seven radial paths, central circular square and outer promenade are the project's current spatial layout. No independently validated navigation implementation is claimed.
